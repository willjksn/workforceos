# Content guide

Stable marketing copy lives in `sites/pierone/lib/content.ts` and page files. Do not invent statistics, client logos, testimonials, or team bios.

WorkforceOS owns jobs, applications, candidates, Transition Talent Profiles (`skillbridge_profiles`), CRM inquiries after submission, and **frequently changing public operational content** (`public_content_items`): featured jobs, featured SkillBridge-eligible employer opportunities, homepage banners, urgent hiring notices, temporary announcements, and industry campaigns.

Do not use WorkforceOS Public Content as a general website editor. About, services, industries structure, legal pages, and permanent brand copy stay in the website codebase.

Public site pages omit a section when no active content is returned. They must not fail.

Do not describe Pier 24 Partners as a high-volume staffing agency, as a generic SkillBridge host program, or promise SkillBridge approval or placement.

The public site leads with three services:

1. Professional & Technical Search
2. Military Talent Opportunity Assessment
3. Workforce Pipeline Assessment

Talent Acquisition Performance Assessment and Fractional Talent Partner stay in the service catalog and keep their URLs. They are not in the public menu, homepage, footer, or contact choices.

Primary industries include Honeywell Technologies as a market focus, not as a claim of a current client engagement.

Insights stay empty until approved articles exist.

Publishing process, roles, scheduling, and cache: `docs/public-site/PUBLIC_CONTENT_PUBLISHING.md`.
